Fix pronunciation by merging the fine-tune back toward base IndicF5

The fine-tuned model had the right voice and the wrong Marathi: थांबलं read as
थांबला, लायब्ररी mangled, कोल्हापूर with a ळ, अनन्या as "anya". The errors were
scattered - a different word each render - and a day of pipeline work on
chunking, durations, seeds and edges never touched them.

The diagnosis was wrong. I read the low training counts (थांबलं appears twice in
1.26 hours of transcripts) as "never learned it" and concluded the fix was more
recordings. But IndicF5 was already trained on hundreds of hours of Marathi and
knew these words. 80 epochs over 1.26 hours did not fail to teach them - it
overwrote them. Catastrophic forgetting, and the signature fits exactly: voice
correct, language degraded.

tools/merge_ckpt.py interpolates the weights back toward the base:

    merged = alpha * finetuned + (1 - alpha) * base

Speaker identity sits in a small low-rank part of the weights while general
pronunciation is spread across all of them, so a partial blend keeps the voice
and recovers the language. No training, no GPU, no new recordings.

alpha 0.50 is the pick, listening verdict "all cha, la and kolhapur pronounce
were on point" with the voice still "90% on point". Saved as
model_voice_merged50.pt and now the default in run_queue.py and overnight.bat.
0.70 and 0.85 still mispronounced; 0.30 lost too much voice.

Converting the base for the merge: it ships as model.safetensors with keys
prefixed ema_model._orig_mod. plus a bundled vocoder.*. Strip only _orig_mod.
and drop the vocoder, KEEP ema_model. - then all 364 tensors match the
fine-tune by name and shape. Stripping ema_model. as well gives zero matches,
which is the first thing I tried.

Also in this change: run_queue.py gains --cool-below / --cool-max-min to pause
between stories, and --lock-seed to use one seed for every chunk rather than
seed+i. overnight.bat runs the queue from a Windows Scheduled Task with no
trigger, so it survives closing the terminal.
